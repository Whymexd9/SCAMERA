SCAMERA uses the camera to take photos and videos, and the microphone only to record the sound of a video.
SCAMERA needs access to storage to save photos, videos and RAW files on the device (DCIM/Camera, DCIM/SCAMERA).
SCAMERA does not use the location and does not connect to the internet: photos, settings, logs (DCIM/SCAMERA/Log) and
crash reports (Download/SCAMERA) stay on the device and leave it only when the user shares them.
